Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

1. General

These terms apply to appointments and purchases made through the Steelcut Barber Co. website. By submitting a booking, you confirm that the information supplied is accurate and that you accept these terms.

2. Appointments

Appointments are subject to availability and are only offered during published operating hours. Sunday is closed and cannot be booked. A booking is only confirmed after the website accepts the appointment and displays a confirmation.

3. Appointment times

The selected service duration is used when checking availability. A barber cannot be assigned to two appointments that overlap. If a requested barber is already booked, that time is unavailable for that barber.

4. Arrival and late appointments

Please arrive on time. A late arrival may reduce the available service time. If the delay prevents the service from being completed, the appointment may be treated as a missed appointment.

5. Cancellations and rescheduling

Customers should provide at least 4 hours' notice when cancelling or rescheduling. Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may require a deposit or other booking restriction for future appointments.

6. Prices

Displayed prices apply to the listed service and duration. If a service changes materially, the customer will be informed before additional work is performed. Prices may be updated for future bookings.

7. Products

Product descriptions are provided for general grooming guidance. Customers should check the product label and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

8. Conduct

Steelcut may refuse service where a customer's behaviour creates a safety, harassment or disruption concern for staff or other customers.

9. Privacy

Booking details are used to manage appointments, communicate with customers and maintain booking records. Information should not be sold for unrelated marketing purposes.

10. Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time. The version published on the website at the time of booking applies to that booking.

11. Legal notice

This website wording is a practical business template. Before launch, the shop should review its final Terms, Privacy Notice and Refund Policy against its actual operations and applicable South African law.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Services

Where a service has already been completed, refunds are considered only where there is a genuine service issue and should be raised with the shop promptly. Rework may be offered where appropriate.

Cancelled appointments

Appointments cancelled at least 4 hours before the appointment may be rescheduled subject to availability. Late cancellations and no-shows may be non-refundable where a deposit was taken.

Products

Unopened, unused products may be eligible for return subject to the shop's return conditions. Used grooming products are generally not eligible for return for hygiene reasons unless required by applicable law.

This is a website policy template and should be reviewed against the business's actual refund rules and applicable South African law before production use.
